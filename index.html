Privacy Policy

App: The Jewish Widget (הוידג'ט היהודי)
Developer: DoveApp
Last updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The Jewish Widget (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the App. We have built the App to work primarily on your device. We do not operate user accounts and we do not collect or store your personal information on any server.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Summary (Plain Language)

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information.
  • We do not have a server that stores your data. All of your settings and widget configurations are stored locally on your device.
  • We do collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (analytics) to improve the App. This data does not identify you and never includes your health data or your precise location. See Section 4.
  • Health and fitness data (your step count) never leaves your device. It is read from Health Connect only to display the number of steps inside a widget, and it is not sent to us, not shared, and not sold.
  • Location, battery level, alarm time, and similar information are processed locally on your device to render the widgets you choose.

2. Information We Access and How It Is Used

The App can display a variety of optional widget items. Depending on which items you add, the App may access the following data. All of it is processed on your device unless explicitly stated otherwise.

2.1 Health & Fitness Data (Health Connect)

The App offers an optional widget item that displays your daily step count. To show this number, the App reads step-count data from Health Connect (Google’s on-device health data platform), and only after you explicitly grant the relevant permission.

How this data is used:

  • Step-count data is read only to display the number of steps inside the widget on your device.
  • The data is cached locally on your device (in the App’s private storage) for a few minutes so the widget can refresh without querying continuously.
  • The step-count data is never transmitted off your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party, it is not shared, and it is not sold.
  • We do not store your health data on any server and we have no access to it.

You can revoke the App’s access to Health Connect at any time through the Health Connect settings on your device. If access is revoked, the steps item simply stops showing a value; no other functionality is affected.

This use of Health Connect data is limited to the in-widget display feature described above and complies with the Health Connect Permissions policy.

2.2 Location

With your permission, the App uses your device location (coarse and/or fine) to calculate Jewish times (zmanim) and to fetch local weather. Your coordinates are sent to the weather provider (Open-Meteo) solely to retrieve the forecast for your area. We do not store your location on any server. You may instead set a location manually in the App’s settings.

2.3 Other On-Device Information

To render the widget items and click actions you choose, the App may read the following on your device: battery level, the next scheduled alarm, and information about installed apps (used so that tapping a widget can open a relevant app, such as a calendar or weather app). This information is used locally to display the widgets and is not collected or transmitted by us.

2.4 Settings and Configuration

All of your preferences, including which widgets you use and how they are configured, are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to any server.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Weather (Open-Meteo): When you use a weather-related widget item, your approximate coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to obtain the forecast. Please refer to Open-Meteo’s own terms and privacy information.
  • Google Play Billing: If you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.
  • Health Connect: Provided by Google as an on-device platform. See Section 2.1.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Used to collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics. See Section 4.

4. Analytics

The App collects anonymous, aggregated usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google, in order to understand how the App is used and to improve it.

Examples of what is collected:

  • Which widgets are added or removed.
  • Whether the exact-alarm permission was granted.
  • Whether the KWGT integration screen was used.
  • Steps in the purchase flow (for example, paywall views and completed purchases).
  • Non-personal display preferences, such as the date format chosen in the Selective widget.
  • Basic technical and usage information automatically gathered by Google Analytics for Firebase, such as the app version, device model, operating system version, general region (derived from IP address, which is not retained), and in-app events.

This analytics data is anonymous and aggregated. It does not include your name, email, contacts, precise location, or any health or fitness data, and it is not used to identify you personally. We have disabled the collection of advertising identifiers (Advertising ID).

The information is processed by Google acting as our analytics provider; please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles such data. We use this data solely to diagnose problems and improve the App, and we do not sell it.

5. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your data. We do not share your personal or health data with third parties, except for: (a) the limited, functional transfer of your approximate coordinates to the weather provider described in Section 2.2; and (b) the anonymous, aggregated analytics data processed by Google as our analytics provider, described in Section 4. Your health and fitness data is never shared with anyone.

6. Data Retention

Because data is stored locally on your device, it remains only as long as the App is installed or until you clear the App’s data. Uninstalling the App removes the locally stored settings and cached data. Health Connect permissions can be revoked at any time from your device settings.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App does not collect personal information from any user, this is generally not a concern; however, if you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us.

8. Your Choices and Controls

  • You can grant or revoke the Location permission at any time in your device settings, or set a location manually in the App.
  • You can grant or revoke Health Connect (steps) access at any time through the Health Connect settings on your device.
  • You can remove all locally stored data by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling the App.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
